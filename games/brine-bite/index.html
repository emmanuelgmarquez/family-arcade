BrineBite

Hunt the open ocean, keep your health up by eating, and climb the food chain. Coins you grab are yours to keep for new sharks and upgrades.

Reef Runner

How to play
  • Point the mouse where you want to swim, or use Arrows/WASD. Hold click or Space to boost. P pauses.Touch anywhere and drag like a joystick. Hold Boost to sprint.
  • Anything shorter than about 85% of your body is food. Bigger sharks hunt you until you outgrow them.
  • Chain bites into combos. Combos of x5 or more pay out a bonus when they end.
  • Fill the Frenzy meter to turn gold: invincible, bigger, triple points.
  • Boost through the surface to leap. Air bites score double.
  • Golden light beams on the seabed mark sunken treasure chests.
  • Finish all three missions to raise your Hunter level and permanent score multiplier.

Each shark plays differently. Bigger sharks start further up the food chain.

Upgrades apply to every shark.
