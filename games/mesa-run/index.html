Mesa Run

MESA RUN

Dilla the armadillo has mail to deliver. Cross four stages, dodge the locals, and take down Scorch the gila at the end of Cinder Fort.

Turn your phone sideways to play full screen.

  • ←→ move
  • ZSpace jump, tap again in midair to double jump
  • XShift run, throw fireflies when powered
  • ↓ dig into a burrow
  • Enter start
  • P pause
  • M sound

FIELD GUIDE

  • DESERT GEM10 points. Fifty of them earn an extra life.
  • SUPPLY CRATEBump it from below for gems and power-ups. Some keep paying out gems as long as you keep bumping.
  • GOLD FEATHERDilla grows bigger, smashes sandstone bricks, and can take one hit before shrinking back.
  • FIREFLY JARMakes Dilla big and lets him throw glowing fireflies with X or Shift (B on touch). A hit takes the fireflies away.
  • THORNBACK BEETLEStomp it, or bump the block it's standing on.
  • SUNSTONEBounces out of rare crates. Grab it for ten seconds of invincibility.
  • PRICKLY PEARHidden inside invisible blocks. Jump into the right empty spot to find an extra life.
  • BURROW MOUNDSome mounds lead underground. Stand on top and press Down to dig into a tunnel full of gems.
  • DUST DEVILHides in a few crates. Bump it loose and jump into the swirl to ride up to a cloud bonus area.
  • PILL BUGStomp it and it curls into a ball. Touch the ball to kick it through other enemies.
  • EMBER MOTHLoops through the air. Land on it from above.
  • TRAIL CAIRNTouch it and you'll restart here if you lose a life.
  • SCORCHThe gila who guards Cinder Fort. Stomp him for two hits or pelt him with fireflies for one. Jump his fireballs.
